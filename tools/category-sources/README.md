# Category icon sources

**Nothing in here is loaded by the addon.** These are the BG Wiki originals the
17 region badges are derived from, and the tool that fetches them. The badges the
panel actually draws are the 64 px derivatives under `assets/category/`,
generated by `tools/make_category_icons.py`.

```
fetch_icons.py             downloads the originals into icons/
icons/                     the originals, plus manifest.json naming the source
                           URL of every one
badges_vs_originals.png    every shipped badge beside its source, written by
                           make_category_icons.py on every run
```

`icons/*.png` is not in the repository: those are BG Wiki's files rather than
this addon's, and `fetch_icons.py` gets them again. `icons/manifest.json` is,
because it is the attribution record and the input the build reads.

## Where the icons come from, and how

The two category pages are **not** re-fetched: questmarks already caches them as
rendered Parsoid HTML under `questmarks/bgwiki-rest-cache/directories/`. The
mapping from group to icon is the wiki's own, because each icon is the `<img>`
inside the anchor that links to that sub-category, so nothing here is a guess
about which picture belongs to which category.

Only the image files themselves need the network, because questmarks' cache
deliberately skips the `File:` namespace. They are fetched from
`https://www.bg-wiki.com` following the conventions in
`questmarks/tools/pipeline/fetch_wiki.py`: a descriptive User-Agent, one request
at a time, a second between them, and nothing refetched that is already on disk.

## What the sources are like, and why the build is not a copy

**The wiki's category images are two different kinds of picture.** The nations,
San d'Oria, Bastok and Windurst, and Jeuno are flat heraldic **crests**, and they
are still themselves at 16 px. The expansions are **cover art**: a face for Wings
of the Goddess, a party of six for The Voracious Resurgence, a shadowed figure
for Campaign. At 16 px those are a coloured smudge, and a row is 22 px tall, so
16 to 18 px is the whole budget. There is no size at which the cover art reads.

**Two gaps in the source data, and the panel handles both:**

- **Coalition** has no icon on either category page, so it draws none and keeps
  its label column.
- **Rise of the Zilart** has no icon of its own: the wiki's Category:Missions
  page uses **Jeuno's crest** for it, so the two share a badge here as they do
  there.

Most of the originals are thumbnails on a flat white or black plate with the
corners rounded off. `make_category_icons.py` lifts that plate where it can do so
without touching the artwork, and refuses the cut where it cannot. Jeuno's is the
one that keeps its plate: its emblem is an open triangle whose interior white is
continuous with the card, so a corner fill walks straight in through the gaps.
`badges_vs_originals.png` is written every run, original above and generated
below, on a light ground and a dark one, so that class of fault is caught by
looking rather than by playing.

## The licence, which is not a footnote here

BG Wiki content is **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**, and these files are wiki *images* of
Square Enix artwork, whose copyright is a separate question from the wiki's own
licence on its text. `NOTICE` states that in full and carries the attribution;
`assets/category/SOURCES.json` records the source file, the source URL and the
wiki categories behind every shipped badge.

If it ever needs to be avoided entirely, the flat crests are simple enough to
redraw, and the launcher icons show how an original is commissioned for this
addon.
